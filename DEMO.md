# Titip Demo Video Script (< 3 Minutes)

Target runtime: **2 minutes 40 seconds** (~365 spoken words).
Use the built-in sample lesson (*Fractions: half and quarter*) and a real phone switched to Flight Mode. Do not claim learning gains; keep the made-up class clearly described as demonstration data.

| Time | Visual / Shot List | Voiceover (VO) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:25 | **1. Problem & File Transfer.** Screen recording of `titip.rofihosted.space/app/#/make`. Show the language & topic filters (`12 lessons`, including **TaRL Numeracy Diagnostic** and `العربية` RTL), then click **Download lesson file** to save `fractions-half-and-quarter.html` (~198 KB raw, ~66 KB compressed)—or tap **Copy text code** for a `TITIP1:` string. Cut to sharing via Bluetooth or WhatsApp. | "Across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, 88 to 95% of school-age children lack internet at home—and ~3.4 billion people remain offline globally. With Titip, a teacher picks a diagnostic or lesson in any of nine languages and downloads one self-contained HTML file—or copies one `TITIP1:` code—that travels offline by Bluetooth, WhatsApp, or SMS." |
| 0:25 – 0:45 | **2. Flight Mode Open.** Receiving phone: pull down Quick Settings, turn **Flight Mode ON** (Wi-Fi and mobile data off). Open **Files**, tap `fractions-half-and-quarter.html`, open in Chrome. Point to the green **Offline: still works** chip, the inline cake fraction SVG diagram, and the worked example. Tap **equal** -> `equal: the same size`. Toggle **A+** and tap **Listen**. | "On the learner's phone, Flight Mode is on—zero internet. Tapping the file opens the lesson with an inline SVG diagram and worked example. Learners tap underlined words for meanings, enlarge text, or press Listen to hear it read aloud." |
| 0:45 – 1:15 | **3. Lesson with Wrong Answer & Typed Input Scaffold.** Tap **Start practice**. On Q1, pick **One quarter** (wrong on purpose) -> red banner + one-sentence hint, then pick **One half**. Answer Q2 and Q3 right. On Q4 (*8 mangoes, half*), type `5` wrongly first to show typed-input scaffolding reveal choices, then select **4**. Answer Q1 again under **One more try**. | "Pick a wrong answer and Titip disables that choice with a one-sentence hint. Q4 starts as a typed number without multiple-choice guessing—and if stuck, it falls back to choices on retry. Missed questions return once more at the end." |
| 1:15 – 1:35 | **4. TaRL Band, Result Code & Shared-Phone Queue.** Show `You got 2 of 4 right on the first try`, **Level: L3 · Building**, and the result code (**`HI-1130-2`** for this run: digit `0` marks a typed answer that matched no choice). Tap **Save to phone queue**, then **Next learner** so a sibling can complete a turn, showing **Send all by SMS** with both codes queued. | "Titip gives an estimated level band plus an anonymous result code like `HI-1132-0`. Families sharing one phone can queue multiple siblings' turns locally and batch-send all codes in a single SMS." |
| 1:35 – 1:58 | **5. Printable Worksheet (1-up & 2-up Slips).** Switch to **Make** -> show **Print worksheet** (upside-down answer key) and **Print 2-up slips (half paper)** with `My answers: ____` and `Total: ____` (`2 1 3 2 = 8`). | "No phone at home? Print the worksheet with an upside-down answer key—or print 2-up half-paper slips to cut apart and cut paper costs in half. Written totals catch single miscopied digits." |
| 1:58 – 2:20 | **6. Results Table & TaRL Grouping.** Open **Results**. Type `HI-1132-0`, `1132 7`, and `3132 9`, or tap **Fill in a made-up class** (keeping *"Made-up data for demonstration"* visible). Point to **Practice groups (TaRL-style, by quiz score)** (Group A `<50%`, B `50–79%`, C `80–100%`), Question 1's **Teach again** badge, and per-choice error chips. | "Back in class, Titip parses SMS codes and paper totals together, automatically sorts learners into TaRL-style practice groups A, B, and C by score, highlights weak questions as 'Teach again', and shows exact wrong-choice counts." |
| 2:20 – 2:40 | **7. Follow-up Lesson & Honest Close.** Tap **Make a follow-up lesson from the "Teach again" questions**, showing `Fractions: half and quarter 2` pre-filled with just the weak question(s). Cut to the two-round comparison bars (`First round` vs `Second round` after `---`). End on `titip.rofihosted.space` landing page. | "One tap builds a follow-up lesson from only the missed questions. After teaching again, adding new codes below `---` compares both rounds side by side. Logic and browser flows are checked; real-learner testing comes next via the 30-minute field protocol in `TEST.md`." |
