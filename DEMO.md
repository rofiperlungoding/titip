# Titip Demo Video Script (< 3 Minutes)

Target runtime: **2 minutes 40 seconds** (~365 spoken words).
Use the built-in sample lesson (*Fractions: half and quarter*) and a real phone switched to Flight Mode. Do not claim learning gains; keep the made-up class clearly described as demonstration data.

| Time | Visual / Shot List | Voiceover (VO) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:25 | **1. Problem & File Transfer.** Screen recording of `titip.rofihosted.space/app/#/make`. Show the language switcher (`9 languages`, including `العربية` RTL and Swahili/Hindi/Bengali lessons in **Lessons**), then click **Download lesson file** to save `fractions-half-and-quarter.html` (~148 KB raw, ~52 KB compressed). Cut to sharing that single `.html` file via Bluetooth or WhatsApp (attached as Document). | "Across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, 88 to 95% of school-age children lack internet at home—and ~3.4 billion people remain offline globally. When practice lives behind a link, learners miss out. With Titip, a teacher writes a lesson once—in any of nine languages, including Arabic RTL—and downloads one self-contained HTML file that travels phone to phone by Bluetooth, WhatsApp, or memory card." |
| 0:25 – 0:45 | **2. Flight Mode Open.** Receiving phone: pull down Quick Settings, turn **Flight Mode ON** (Wi-Fi and mobile data off). Open **Files**, tap `fractions-half-and-quarter.html`, open in Chrome. Point to the green **Offline: still works** chip in the top bar. Tap the underlined word **equal** to reveal `equal: the same size`. Toggle **A+** (larger text) and tap **Listen**. | "On the learner's phone, Flight Mode is on—zero internet. Tapping the file opens the lesson straight in the browser. A learner can tap underlined words for plain meanings, enlarge text, or press Listen to hear the lesson read aloud using the phone's built-in voice." |
| 0:45 – 1:15 | **3. Lesson with One Wrong Answer.** Tap **Start practice**. On Question 1 (*"You cut a cake into 2 equal parts. What is one part called?"*), select **One quarter** (wrong on purpose) and tap **Check answer**. Show the red banner: `✗ Not yet. Try another answer. Hint: Half means 2 equal parts.` Then select **One half** (`✓ Correct`). Answer Q2 (**1/2**), Q3 (**2**), Q4 (**4**) right on the first try. Show Q1 returning at the end under **One more try** and answer **One half**. | "Now practice starts. Pick a wrong answer on question one, and Titip disables that choice and shows a one-sentence hint instead of just 'wrong'. Pick the right answer to move on. Questions two, three, and four go smoothly—and at the end, the missed question comes back once more for another try." |
| 1:15 – 1:35 | **4. Result Code.** Show the final screen: `You got 3 of 4 right on the first try.` Zoom on the result code **`HI-1132-0`**. Tap **Send by SMS** briefly to show the messaging app pre-filled (`HI-1132-0`), return, and tap **Next learner** to show the lesson resetting for another child on a shared phone. | "At the end, Titip gives a short anonymous code: `HI-1132-0`. It stores the lesson ID, the learner's first pick on each question, and a check digit—no name, no account. They can send it by SMS or write it on paper, then tap Next learner for a sibling sharing the phone." |
| 1:35 – 1:58 | **5. Printable Worksheet (No-Phone Path).** Switch to teacher view **Make** -> tap **Print worksheet**. Show print preview (or printed sheet) highlighting numbered choices, the bottom lines (`My answers: ____`, `Total: ____` with `2 1 3 2` and `8` written in), and the upside-down answer key and hints at the foot. | "What if there is no phone at home? One click prints the same lesson as a paper worksheet, with the answer key and hints upside down at the foot. The learner writes their answer numbers and their sum—like `2 1 3 2 = 8`—so copying mistakes are caught." |
| 1:58 – 2:20 | **6. Results Table.** Open **Results**. Type `HI-1132-0`, `1132 7`, and `3132 9` on separate lines and tap **Show results**; then tap **Fill in a made-up class** (keeping the *"Made-up data for demonstration"* banner visible) to show a 12-learner two-round table with `---`. Point to Question 1's red **Teach again** badge, the classroom hint, **Most common wrong answer: One quarter**, and the per-choice error chips underneath. | "Back in class, the teacher enters SMS codes and paper totals into the same box. Here is a made-up 12-learner example to show how the table reads: Titip highlights Question 1 as 'Teach again', shows the exact wrong-answer counts per choice, and puts the hint right beside it." |
| 2:20 – 2:40 | **7. Follow-up Lesson & Honest Close.** Tap **Make a follow-up lesson from the "Teach again" questions**, showing `Fractions: half and quarter 2` pre-filled with just the weak question(s). Cut to the two-round comparison bars (`First round` vs `Second round` after `---`). End on `titip.rofihosted.space` landing page. | "One tap builds a follow-up lesson from only the missed questions. After teaching again, adding new codes below `---` compares both rounds side by side. Logic and browser flows are checked; real-learner testing comes next via the 30-minute field protocol in `TEST.md`." |
